Rules of the challenge

Play Fair, Win Big.

Everything you need to know before you join — eligibility, schedule, points and verification, all in one place.

Eligibility

Any live online store (Shopify or otherwise) with a public storefront can enter. One entry per store. You must own the rights to the logo you submit and grant Glood a one-week licence to display it. Glood staff, agencies working for Glood, and their stores are excluded.

Round 1 Schedule

Winning

The top 10 brands by verified points at close take the 10 spots. Rank decides the spot: #1 is the top-centre sticker, then clockwise. Ties go to whoever reached the score first.

Verification

Submit links via the join form or the follow-up email. Glood verifies within 48 hours; unverified tasks count for zero. The leaderboard updates daily.

Not Allowed

  • Purchased followers or engagement
  • Multiple entries for one store
  • Offensive or misleading content
  • Logos you do not own the rights to
  • Shopify App Store reviews — these earn no points, as Shopify prohibits incentivised reviews

Any of the above removes the entry.

The Fine Print

No purchase or payment is required or accepted to participate. Glood's decisions on verification and disputes are final. Glood may edit the schedule with notice on this page.
